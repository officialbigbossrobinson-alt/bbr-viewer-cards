Battles

In the middle of a match? Pause stops the countdown until you press Resume (nothing starts while paused). Delay pushes the next battle back. Mods can do the same in chat: !battle pause, !battle resume, !battle delay 10. A Stream Deck button can do it too (links below).

How each battle works

Penalty Shootout (2 players vs the AI keeper): type L C or R on your kick, no pick = a miss. Chat puts them off with !boo !horn !flash !laser !flare !firework or backs them with !chant. Watch the composure meter.
Truck Race (up to 8 drivers): !turbo and !dodge. Chat uses !rain !wind !lightning !traffic: nobody picks a target, it usually hits the leader.
Queen Raid (everyone plays): !fire !grenade !heal. Cheers and subs heal raiders and raise the team's win chance.

Join lasts 30s (the shootout starts early with 2 players), then 15s to boost. Card points (each = +1 to the lowest stat, max 5 a day): shootout 3 / 1 · race 3 / 2 / 1 · Queen Raid won: MVP 3, 2nd-3rd 2, everyone who scored 1. Mods can type !battle, !battle shootout, !battle race or !battle queen.

Twitch clips setup

What works best (OBS › Settings):

  1. Reconnect Twitch once (Setup › Twitch › Connect Twitch) and say yes: the app now asks to make and download clips.
  2. Clips only work while you're live on Twitch (Kick and YouTube still see the replay on your stream).
  3. A clip is ready about 15–60 seconds after you tap: the dock shows "Twitch is making it", then plays it.
  4. Downloaded clips are kept in the app's data/clips folder and stay on your Twitch channel as normal clips.

Replay load test

Plays your newest clip as a full test replay and watches OBS's own numbers at every step (opening the clip, moving the game, playing, slow-mo, back to live). It shows exactly where OBS drops frames, and checks the OBS settings that usually cause it. Takes about 40 seconds; the clip is kept.

Most accurate: test while OBS is really streaming, because the stream encoder adds load. To do that without going live on Twitch: OBS › Settings › Stream, add ?bandwidthtest=true to the end of your stream key, press Start Streaming (Twitch doesn't show it to anyone), run the test with your game running, then Stop Streaming and remove it from the key again.

Two yellows and a straight red give the player a ban (numbers in Settings). The match it happens in doesn't count: each result you log after that takes one match off. Banned players are skipped on the Matchday squad team sheet.

Beat the Boss

One viewer against you in a penalty shootout. Chat types !join, one name is drawn (subs first if you want). They shoot with L / C / R, you shoot from here, the dock or the Stream Deck. AI keepers in both goals. You press L / C / R here, on the dock, phone or Stream Deck. Too busy? Don't press: the AI picks for you, in your usual style.
