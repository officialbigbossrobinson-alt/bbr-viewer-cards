YOUR OWN TEXTURES (optional)
============================
The penalty stadium paints its own high-resolution textures, so this folder can stay empty.

If you want photo-real ones, drop images in here with these names (jpg, png or webp, up to 4K):

  grass.jpg    close-up of pitch grass, seen from above. It is tiled, so a seamless texture works best.
  crowd.jpg    a stand full of fans, seen from the front.
  crowd2.jpg   (optional) the same crowd a moment later (people jumping). The two swap to make the crowd bounce.
  sky.jpg      night sky above the stadium.

Restart the app (or refresh the overlay in OBS) after adding files.
Only use images you own or have the rights to show on stream.
